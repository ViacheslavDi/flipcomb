How do I play?

Drag pieces from below the field onto the comb. Tap a piece to turn it, tap twice to flip it. Each piece's symbol must land on the same symbol of the field. The full rules are in the app: tap “?” in a level.

A purchase did not arrive

Open the honey shop and tap “Restore purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I turn off ads?

Premium removes the ads between retries for good. Videos for a drop of honey are always your choice.

How do I turn off reminders?

Settings in the app → “Daily reminder”, or iOS Settings → Notifications → Flipcomb.

My progress is gone

Progress is stored on the device. Deleting the app deletes it too. Purchases can always be restored.
