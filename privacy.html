Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

In short

Flipcomb has no accounts and no analytics of its own. Your progress stays on your device. Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which may collect data as described below. Premium removes the ads between retries.

Your game on your device

Solved levels, honey drops, settings and purchases are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We have no server and do not receive them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Ads by Google AdMob

The free game shows ads from Google AdMob: a short break after several retries of one level and videos you choose to watch for a drop of honey. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), IP address, coarse location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in the app's settings. If you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalised. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Honey packs and Premium are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only learns which item you bought, so it can deliver it and restore it later.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a daily reminder on your device itself. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app's settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

Flipcomb is a puzzle for everyone, but it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad requests can be marked for child-directed treatment where the law requires it.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no tracking. The demo remembers your level only in your own browser's storage.

Your rights

You can ask us any question about your data, and use your rights to access, correct or delete data under GDPR and similar laws. Data collected by Google for ads is managed by Google; you can reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings or turn tracking off at any time.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app changes how it handles data, and change the date above. Write to us at flipcomb.game@gmail.com.
